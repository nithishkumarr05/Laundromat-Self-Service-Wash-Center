Our Story

How We Started

From a simple idea to a trusted community laundry center

FreshWash was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to make laundry convenient, affordable, and eco-friendly. What started as a small self-service laundromat has grown into a full-service laundry center serving hundreds of satisfied customers.

2024 — The Beginning

Opened our first location with 10 state-of-the-art machines

2025 — Expansion

Added wash & dry service and introduced subscription plans

2026 — Today

500+ happy customers, 24/7 service, and eco-friendly commitment
